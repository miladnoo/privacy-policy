Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026  ·  Effective: September 9, 2026

The individual developer (“we,” “us,” or “our”) operates the mobile application Classic Garage (the “App”). Classic Garage is a local maintenance log for cars: you keep a garage of vehicles, service history, receipts, and related photos on your device. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, why we collect it, how it is used and shared, and the choices you have. By downloading or using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide to Us

  • Garage records. Year, make, model, VIN, mileage, engine, service dates, descriptions, costs, part numbers, notes, and photos you attach of cars or receipts. This information is stored in a database on your device. The App works without an account.
  • VIN decode. If you enter or scan a VIN, that VIN is sent to the U.S. National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA) vPIC API so the App can fill year, make, and model.
  • Account information. If you create an account, we collect the information you provide during registration, including an email address and password if you sign up with email, or the name and email address that Apple or Google share with us if you use Sign in with Apple or Google. If you do not create an account, this does not apply to you.
  • Optional cloud backup. If you sign in and use cloud sync, copies of car and service text may be stored in Firebase (Google Cloud). Photos are intended to stay on your device unless a photo upload succeeds; failed photo uploads remain local.
  • Purchases and subscriptions. If you make a purchase or subscribe through the App, the transaction is processed by the app store or payment processor you used (Apple App Store, via RevenueCat). We do not collect or store full payment card numbers, and we never see your card details. We receive whether your Lifetime or Monthly unlock is active.
  • Communications. If you contact us for support, we collect your email address and the contents of your message so we can respond.

1.2 Information We Collect Automatically

When you use the App, we do not run a separate analytics or crash-reporting product of our own. The services listed in Section 5 may automatically receive technical information needed to operate those services, including device type, operating system, app version, and authentication or purchase events. We do not collect advertising identifiers for advertising. This information helps operate, secure, and provide the App and is processed by those providers under their own policies.

1.3 Information from Third-Party Services

If you choose to sign in using a third-party service (such as Apple, Google, or another provider), we receive only the basic profile information that service makes available to us and that you authorize, such as your name and email address.

1.4 Device Permissions and Sensitive Information

The App requests the following device permissions and uses the associated information only for the purpose stated:

  • Camera. We access the camera only when you choose to photograph a car or receipt, or to scan a VIN barcode on a dash or door sticker.
  • Photo library. We access your photo library only when you choose a car photo or receipt from existing pictures.

The App does not request microphone, location, contacts, or notification permission. We do not collect information that directly identifies you from these permissions beyond what is needed to provide the feature you asked for.

We use the information we collect to:

  • respond to your questions, requests, and support inquiries;
  • monitor, analyze, and improve the App’s performance, stability, and user experience;
  • detect, prevent, and address technical issues, fraud, and abuse; and
  • comply with legal obligations.
